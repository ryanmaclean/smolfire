## conclusion

loop device failed to write its last sectors to the full backing tmpfs; the error was only
logged by the kernel. Same setup on ubrpi502 (Ubuntu 6.8.0-1051-raspi) returned EIO to the
writer and lost nothing. teardown verified: 0 dbg mounts, 0 eio.img loop devices.
