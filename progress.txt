## Codebase Patterns
- Coordinator per-task HALT recovery must clear both `halted_tasks` and `var/mail/HALT.<task_id>`.
- Resume headers are `X-Resume-Tag` and `X-Resume-Action`; tag format is `resume-<task_id>`.
- Per-task resume handling should run even when global `var/mail/HALT` is absent.
- FSM transitions and harvested verdicts must use the fixed telemetry schema emitted by `log-transition` and `log-verdict` in `bin/coord-tick.nu`.
- Attestation policy is inherited from the full `In-Reply-To` chain; reply-local `attestation_required` is not authoritative by itself.
- Dispatch capability checks and executor selection happen before a request enters `dispatching`; explicit unsupported executors are refused rather than silently downgraded.
- Image-release builds use `cloudware-release` with the real `SMOLFIRECONF`/`CLOUDWARE` configuration; do not resurrect the old `vm-image ... CLOUDWARE_CONF` path.
- Runtime data under `var/` is local state/spool and must not be committed.
